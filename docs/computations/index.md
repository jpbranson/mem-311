# Attested computations

* [Volume and resolution time](volume-and-resolution.md) - Requests opened and closed, and median and P90 resolution time in days, over all dates and categories.
* [Resolved within 7 and 30 days](resolved-within-days.md) - Share of requests resolved within 7 and 30 days, counting only opening cohorts old enough to have been observed that long.
* [Backlog at the data cut-off](backlog-snapshot.md) - Open requests, share older than 30 and 90 days, median and P90 age, and count older than 180 days on the last complete day.
* [Recurrence rates](recurrence-rates.md) - Primary-definition recurrence rate at 30, 90 and 180 days over right-censored originals, and the median days to first recurrence.
* [Persistent and chronic location counts](persistent-location-counts.md) - Number of Persistent-or-Chronic locations and of Chronic locations among places with three or more condition reports.
