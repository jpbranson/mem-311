# Responsiveness

* [Requests opened and closed](request-volume.md) - Requests reported in a period, requests closed in a period by closure date, and the net change between them.
* [Resolution time](resolution-time.md) - Median and 90th-percentile days from report to closure, for requests closed in the period, excluding imputed, invalid and mass closures.
* [Resolved within 7 and 30 days](resolved-within-days.md) - Share of requests reported in a period that closed within 7 or 30 calendar days, over cohorts old enough to have been observed that long.

# Backlog

* [Open backlog](open-backlog.md) - Requests open at the end of a day, split by age band, with shares older than 30 and 90 days and year-over-year change.
* [Backlog age](backlog-age.md) - Median and 90th-percentile age in days of the requests open on the snapshot date, precomputed per category and for all categories.

# Durability

* [Recurrence rate](recurrence-rate.md) - Share of eligible closed condition reports followed by a related report at the same place within 30, 90 or 180 days, under the primary definition.
* [Persistent locations](persistent-locations.md) - Counts of Persistent and Chronic locations, recurrence cycles, and the share of cycles that happen at persistent locations.
