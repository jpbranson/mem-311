# Findings

* [System at a glance](system-at-a-glance.md) - Headline figures: 405,398 requests, 6.6-day median resolution, 21,181 open, 22.1% of eligible closures recur within 90 days.
* [F1 — Closing fast and staying fixed are different things](F1-fast-is-not-durable.md) - Sewer backups close in half a day but 21% return within 90 days; across categories speed barely predicts durability (rank correlation −0.25).
* [F2 — Missed collection is the single largest source of repeat work](F2-missed-collection-drives-repeat-work.md) - Missed garbage, recycling and bulk pickups produce 58% of 90-day recurrences and 73% of chronic locations; without them the recurrence rate falls from 22.1% to 15.7%.
* [F3 — Old backlog keeps accumulating whatever the total does](F3-old-backlog-keeps-growing.md) - After the 2025-09-22 mass closure cut the backlog by two thirds, requests older than 180 days grew by about 190 a month whether the total fell or rose.
* [F4 — A small share of places generates a large share of repeat activity](F4-repeat-activity-is-concentrated.md) - The 4.4% of repeat locations in the Chronic tier account for 29% of recurrence cycles; Chronic and Persistent together (19%) account for 64%.
* [F5 — Recurrence varies more within the city than between districts](F5-durability-varies-within-the-city.md) - Council districts differ by 5 points in 90-day recurrence, while Census tracts with 500+ eligible closures range from 12.8% to 35.9%.
* [What was checked and not found](checked-and-not-found.md) - No measurable shift to AI pothole detection, and closures without work do not explain the recurrence rate.
